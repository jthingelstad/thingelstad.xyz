The nnxyz Rooms is a gallery of the art Jamie collected from nnxyz, an artist on Nifty Ink, a free drawing board on the xDai chain that kept every stroke. As the gallery’s [story of the collector](https://nnxyz.thingelstad.xyz/story/the-collector/) tells it, he found Nifty Ink in March 2022, bought his first nnxyz drawing, Nifty Faces #109, on April 14, 2022, and kept buying until he held four complete series. Along with 646 nnxyz works the gallery holds 26 by Scrimshaw.

The works hang in 30 rooms across six collections, each with room texts, artwork labels, and its own audio tour, and a [map](https://nnxyz.thingelstad.xyz/map/) shows the collection as one building on three floors. Because Nifty Ink recorded every stroke, each drawing is rebuilt as a native vector image that you can enlarge, download, or replay exactly as it was drawn. Story pages cover [Nifty Ink](https://nnxyz.thingelstad.xyz/story/nifty-ink/), which Austin Griffith opened in the summer of 2020 and where nnxyz arrived in October 2021, along with the collector, Jamie’s fan art, the artist’s project, the year 2022, and the artist’s own words.

The collector’s introduction on the front page is Jamie’s. The [About page](https://nnxyz.thingelstad.xyz/about/) says the rest of the gallery text is unsigned, written from the works, their archived records, the public ledgers, and the artist’s posts. He built the site in October 2026 with Astro, from the archived drawings and their on-chain records.

Why this art got a museum, in his words:

> I felt like his work had a lot of emotion and movement and feeling, and I felt like that it told a story. That’s why I bought the collections when I had the opportunity.
>
> [Jamie, question 23](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q23)

Buying on Nifty Ink was, for a family that buys art at art fairs, [“the crypto version of what we always do at art fairs”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q23). And the museum is for him first: [“even if nobody else came there, The fact that I can now visit that museum and explore and see the art is to me really valuable”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q23). He counts it, with Proof of a Person, as [“a new class of thing, something where it’s an activity I did, and I kind of created that as a capstone that will just exist in perpetuity”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q35).
