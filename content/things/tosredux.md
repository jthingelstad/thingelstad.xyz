“A decade or so ago I got the wiki bug in a bad way,” I wrote in [Weekly Thing 306](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/306/), “and one of the use cases I thought made sense was a wiki approach to Terms of Service. I got it started on TOSredux.org with the thought that you could crowdsource a semantic analysis of terms of service and then use a browser plugin to give good feedback to people on what they are accepting.”

The oldest copy of tosredux.org in the Internet Archive is from September 2011. It ran on my MediaWiki farm, and WikiApiary still listed it among the farm’s active wikis in [October 2015](https://web.archive.org/web/20151013153210/https://wikiapiary.com/wiki/Farm:Thingelstad.com). As far as I can tell it went away with the rest of the farm in 2016, when I did my technical downsizing. The idea still makes sense to me.
