“A decade or so ago I got the wiki bug in a bad way,” Jamie wrote in [Weekly Thing 306](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/306/), “and one of the use cases I thought made sense was a wiki approach to Terms of Service. I got it started on TOSredux.org with the thought that you could crowdsource a semantic analysis of terms of service and then use a browser plugin to give good feedback to people on what they are accepting.”

The oldest copy of tosredux.org in the Internet Archive is from September 2011. It ran on his MediaWiki farm, and WikiApiary still listed it among the farm’s active wikis in [October 2015](https://web.archive.org/web/20151013153210/https://wikiapiary.com/wiki/Farm:Thingelstad.com). As far as the record shows it went away with the rest of the farm in 2016, in his technical downsizing. Of running that farm by then, he said: [“It was no longer about learning, which is why I do all this stuff. It had become an administrative chore.”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/session-7/#q52)

How far it got, as he remembers it: [“It never got very far, and I don’t think there were ever any other editors.”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/session-7/#q48) He said the markup and the categories were settled, and entries were made for a number of sites, though not with the terms themselves: [“We got to the clarity that those are copyrighted, so you couldn’t just copy them into the wiki”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/session-7/#q48). What stopped it was the browser.

> The blocker that kept me from pursuing it further was that it seemed really difficult to get it into the browser. The thought was a browser extension, so that when you went to a website you could see its privacy rating in real time, and at the time that just didn’t seem feasible.
>
> [Jamie, question 48](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/session-7/#q48)

The idea still makes sense to him. [“TOSRedux really is about embracing the privacy values I have.”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/session-4/#q27) [“My belief is that many people have no idea what the terms of service are saying and what they actually mean”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/session-4/#q27), and [“ultimately you have this problem of: does anybody really care”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/session-4/#q27). And then: [“I refuse to give up when it comes to privacy.”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/session-4/#q27)
