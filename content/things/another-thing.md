Another Thing is Jamie’s podcast and his third “thing,” after the blog and the Weekly Thing. The [first episode](https://another.thingelstad.com/2025/10/05/how-do-you-start-a.html), “How do you start a podcast?”, came out on October 5, 2025, and he announced it in [Weekly Thing 329](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/329/) as “the podcast that I’ve been thinking about creating for a couple of years.” In it he talks about a chalkboard sign that told him to “be brave enough to suck at something new,” a story about Dr. John Carlis, whose class he took while studying computer science at the University of Minnesota, and an earlier attempt to turn the Weekly Thing into an AI-narrated show.

That is still the only episode. In [Weekly Thing 346](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/346/) he wrote that he was not giving up on the show, and in November 2026 he said the same thing out loud:

> I have no intention of getting rid of it, and I am still committed to that project. … I know that I can’t just simply sit down in front of a microphone and talk ad hoc. I need to have some kind of a outline that I’m working against, some kind of an interface that helps me record, and so I’m figuring that out.
>
> [Jamie, question 21](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q21)

What he wants it to be is [“a way for me to go deeper on topics that likely appear in the Weekly Thing”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q21), [“kind of a behind the scenes or a director’s commentary, if you will”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q21). In 2026 he built Another Thing Builder to make episodes, then retired it in October 2026 and moved podcast authoring into WT Builder, so the next episode has an easier path. Thingy can search the episode transcripts. Asked what he hopes is still running in twenty years, he named the podcast with the blog and the Weekly Thing, with [“a published history of new podcasts”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q35).
