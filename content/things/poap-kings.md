POAP KINGS (#J2RGCRVG) is a Clash Royale clan that Jamie started in early 2026 with his son Tyler and Tyler’s cousin Levi. Jamie had [taken up the game](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/09/27/after-following-tyler-to-pokmon.html) in September 2025 as a newcomer, trying one of Tyler’s favorite games. In the [clan’s own words](https://poapkings.com/faq/), Raquaza and King Levy had cycled through clans that never quite fit, so the family built the clan it wanted: structure, recognition, and something lasting to show for the grind. Jamie claimed the [POAP KINGS Founder](https://www.thingelstad.com/2026/02/05/poap-at-poap-kings-founder.html) POAP on February 5, 2026, and by July the clan had [reached 50 members](https://www.thingelstad.com/2026/07/26/i-made-this-fun-slab.html) for the first time.

The name comes from POAP: the clan began by publishing POAP collectibles for seasons and milestones. When the POAP company and product stopped operating, the clan kept the name and the meaning it had taken on inside the clan, “Proof of Arena Push.”

Jamie asked Codex to [build the website](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/342/) from the notes the three founders had kept, and it was live quickly; Claude Code did much of the later work, design included. In April 2026 he pointed Claude Design at the repository when a new blog feature had made the design “unruly,” and it produced the current look ([Weekly Thing 345](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/345/)). It grew into an Eleventy site on GitHub Pages with a sortable roster, an interactive data page, member guidance, season recognition, and pages for the clan’s sister clans, Ship It! and Elixir Kings. The roster and war data come from Elixir’s JSON API, and the site promotes the tools the clan built: Elixir and Elixir Drop.
