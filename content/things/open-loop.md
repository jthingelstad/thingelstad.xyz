Open Loop was a podcast Jamie made with Garrick van Buren. They recorded nine episodes, numbered 0 to 8, from [June 1, 2012](https://www.thingelstad.com/projects/open-loop/0-but-no-shoes/) to April 12, 2013, talking about Kubb, beer and the technology side projects they were tinkering with. It was Jamie’s first experiment with podcasting ([Open Loop](https://www.thingelstad.com/projects/open-loop/)).

On [episode 1](https://www.thingelstad.com/projects/open-loop/1-big-fuzzy-dead-cat/) Jamie told the origin stories of Road Sign Math and Planet Kubb. Jamie [put the archive back online](https://www.thingelstad.com/2017/06/10/put-the-archive.html) in June 2017, and in October 2026 it was republished as a complete [podcast feed](https://files.thingelstad.com/projects/openloop/podcast.xml), with speaker-labelled transcripts machine transcribed and cleaned up with AI.
