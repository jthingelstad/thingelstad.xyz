The R/W Book Club is a group of technically minded readers who have met in the Minneapolis-Saint Paul area since April 2003. R/W stands for Read / Write: the members read, and many of them also blog. The club reads about eight books a year, mostly nonfiction, and members take turns picking the next book ([About](https://rwbookclub.com/about/)). Jamie built the website, not the club.

Jamie joined in late 2007, when his friend and former professor John Riedl, who started the club, emailed him about “this small book club of geeky guys” he was part of. Jamie “joined without hesitation” and shared the club with John, Tom, Erik and Dan until John died in 2013 ([Goodbye to my friend, John Riedl](https://www.thingelstad.com/2013/07/21/goodbye-to-my.html)).

The club’s record has taken several forms. In early 2011 it was a MediaWiki site called the [Creative Computer Book Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20110323210256/http://creativecomputerbookclub.com/wiki/Main_Page); that July Jamie [hacked on MediaWiki](https://www.thingelstad.com/2011/07/15/i-had-a-blast-hacking.html) with the book club gang, and by January 2012 the same wiki was the [R/W Book Club](https://web.archive.org/web/20120106214958/http://rwbookclub.com/wiki/Main_Page) at rwbookclub.com. In 2017 he built a [Jekyll site](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/17/) at rwbook.club. The club has also kept its record in a shared Airtable database. The current site is generated by [Oliver](https://rwbookclub.com/oliver/), the club’s agent, and offers the reading record as llms.txt files for agents and the meetings as an iCal feed.
