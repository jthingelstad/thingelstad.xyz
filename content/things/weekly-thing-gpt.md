On February 25, 2025, I [made a custom GPT](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/02/25/weekly-thing-gpt.html) that anyone with a ChatGPT account could use, “loaded with all 310 issues of the Weekly Thing published so far.” I suggested questions like “What is the Weekly Thing view on social media?”, “What is worse, Facebook or TikTok?”, and “Show me a list of articles on X along with the issue they were in,” and shared it in [Weekly Thing 311](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/311/).

It had real limits. In March 2025 I showed it [getting the answer wrong](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/313/) when I asked which issue the Fortune first appeared in. Fourteen months later I launched Thingy, which actually searches the archive and cites its sources, and I compared the two in [Weekly Thing 347](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/347/): the GPT “was grade school level. Thingy is much smarter!” The GPT’s link no longer works.
