POAP went into maintenance mode in March 2026 and wound down that summer. On October 17, 2026 Jamie captured his whole POAP life into a public [archive](https://github.com/jthingelstad/poap-archive): every token he collected, every drop he created, the people who minted them, and the posts and Weekly Thing sections he wrote about POAP from 2021 on. The tokens themselves stay on the Gnosis chain. The archive keeps the records and artwork that lived on POAP’s own services, which would otherwise be gone.

[Proof of a Person](https://poap.thingelstad.xyz/) is the time capsule he built from that archive and published the same day. It counts 259 tokens collected, from the ENS 4th Anniversary vote on June 7, 2021 to an airport rally stop at Seattle-Tacoma in July 2026, and 130 drops issued, which 639 people minted. Each year from 2021 to 2026 is a chapter headed by a line from his own posts. The pages show the timeline as two sides, what he collected and what he made for other people, with a network of the people who hold his drops, his writing about POAP, and a short history of the company.

The site is a static Astro build from a JSON export of the archive. Drop artwork is served from a copy he took from POAP’s asset host while it was still up.

What POAP had been for him, in his words:

> It allowed me to assert that I did a thing without relying on any co company or other kind of independent arbiter to be able to host and own that data. Instead, that data lived on the blockchain and, and really delivered that promise of blockchain technology. … It just became something that I truly, really cherished.
>
> [Jamie, question 15](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q15)

His reading of the end: [“POAP was kind of doomed, I think, in part because crypto has never answered the onboarding problem.”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q15) He counts the capsule, with the nnxyz Rooms, as a new kind of thing, and [“I would expect that there will be more of those things because that’s kind of a new lever”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q35).
