Jiggy Thing Puzzles are custom jigsaw puzzles our family made together. The first, **Jiggy Thing Puzzle #001 “Celebration City,”** is a 500-piece puzzle of hand-drawn digital art packed with little scenes, like an ant hotel with a “no vacancy” sign. On October 6, 2021, I wrote that we had finished the box design and [ordered 50 puzzles](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/10/06/our-family-created.html). We had made puzzles before, but this was our first custom box, and when the order showed up I was happy with how it came out ([Custom Puzzle Box](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/10/24/custom-puzzle-box.html)).

We made the puzzle for the first [Things 4 Good Fall Fundraiser](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/11/09/things-good-fall.html) in November 2021, where it sold alongside our candles. After the sale, [Weekly Thing 203](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/203/) offered the rest for $35, printed on Eska board in the custom box. We sold 26 puzzles in 2021 and 10 in 2022. The 2022 sale offered the same puzzle, so “a number of folks already had it” ([Things 4 Good Fall Fundraiser Notes](https://www.thingelstad.com/2022/11/27/101105.html)).

There hasn’t been a second puzzle yet. My [puzzles page](https://www.thingelstad.com/puzzles/) has said “Coming Soon…” since at least December 2022.
