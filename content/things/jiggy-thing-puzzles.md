Jiggy Thing Puzzles are custom jigsaw puzzles the Thingelstad family made together. The first, **Jiggy Thing Puzzle #001 “Celebration City,”** is a 500-piece puzzle of hand-drawn digital art full of small scenes, such as an ant hotel with a “no vacancy” sign. On October 6, 2021, Jamie wrote that the family had finished the box design and [ordered 50 puzzles](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/10/06/our-family-created.html). The family had made puzzles before, but this was their first custom box, and Jamie was pleased with how it came out when the order arrived ([Custom Puzzle Box](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/10/24/custom-puzzle-box.html)).

The puzzle was made for the first [Things 4 Good Fall Fundraiser](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/11/09/things-good-fall.html) in November 2021, where it sold alongside the family’s candles. After the sale, [Weekly Thing 203](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/203/) offered the remaining puzzles for $35, printed on Eska board in the custom box. The family sold 26 puzzles in 2021 and 10 in 2022. Jamie noted that 2022 offered the same puzzle, so “a number of folks already had it” ([Things 4 Good Fall Fundraiser Notes](https://www.thingelstad.com/2022/11/27/101105.html)).

No second puzzle has been published. Jamie’s [puzzles page](https://www.thingelstad.com/puzzles/) has said “Coming Soon…” since at least December 2022.
