Reading Things was a newsletter about books from the whole Thingelstad family. Tammy, Mazie, Tyler, and Jamie each wrote about what they were reading. Jamie teased it as “a fun, new family project” in [Weekly Thing 177](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/177/), and the [first issue](https://web.archive.org/web/20210508214912/https://reading.thingelstad.com/archive/reading-things-book-thief-atomic-habits-treasure/) went out on May 8, 2021. In [Weekly Thing 186](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/186/) he explained the plan: four emails a year, about books and things related to them, like Tammy’s Corner and a bookstore challenge. No affiliate links, no tracking, and no “buy now” links.

Issues followed in [summer](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/07/25/we-just-sent.html) and [fall](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/10/31/we-just-published.html) 2021. The [Winter 2022 issue](https://www.thingelstad.com/2022/02/06/the-winter-issue.html), sent on February 6, 2022, is the last one in the [archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20211130142318/https://reading.thingelstad.com/archive/) and the last one he mentioned. In July 2021 the family marked 100 subscribers with a [Reading Things First 100 POAP](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/07/25/poap-at-reading-things-first.html), which Jamie [wrote about](https://www.thingelstad.com/2022/06/04/my-introduction-to.html) in June 2022. There was no goodbye issue. It stopped after four, and reading.thingelstad.com no longer answers.
