Stone/Steel/People is Jamie’s [first photo book](https://www.thingelstad.com/2010/12/05/first-photo-book.html). He made it after Layne Kennedy’s 72 Hours workshop at the Minneapolis Photo Center, and the name is what he photographed there: stone, steel, and people. Two weeks before it came out he wrote about [editing it down](https://www.thingelstad.com/2010/11/15/editing-a-book.html) to the pictures that belonged.

The book is square, one image to a page, “a little over 30 pictures,” and it took him “between 10 and 15 hours” to put together. He published it through Blurb, where it is [still for sale](https://www.blurb.com/b/1813441-stone-steel-people). It is archived because it is done. In the interview he grouped it with the tree carving and the puzzle: [“these projects all came to a natural conclusion”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q28).
