Jamie started blogging when writing on the web was becoming a common way to share ideas. He discovered that he enjoyed writing in public: telling stories about his experiences and sharing the projects he was working on. That enjoyment became a habit that continues today.

With roughly 10,000 posts across more than 20 years, the blog is a long-running record of one person’s life. It is personal and noncommercial, bringing together everyday experiences, photographs, and things Jamie builds.

The blog has moved from .Text, a Windows .NET blogging package, through WordPress to Micro.blog. Its writing has expanded from mainly longer articles to include shorter updates and, increasingly, photographs as mobile publishing made sharing them easier. It is a foundation for the wider collection, and its posts are syndicated in Weekly Thing.
