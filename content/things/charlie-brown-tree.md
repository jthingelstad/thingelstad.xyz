The Charlie Brown Tree, which we also call the Peanuts Tree, is a tall tree stump at Magic Pines, our place on Cannon Lake, carved with Peanuts characters climbing toward Snoopy’s red doghouse on top. Adam Gale of [Rust n’ Grain](https://rustngraincom.wordpress.com/) carved it. I posted [the carving in progress](https://www.thingelstad.com/2020/05/24/little-snippet-of.html), Charlie Brown holding Snoopy emerging from the wood, on 2020-05-24, and the tree in the snow in [Weekly Thing 163](https://weekly.thingelstad.com/archive/163/) that October. The [project page](https://www.thingelstad.com/magic-pines/charlie-brown-tree/) has videos.

Logs left over from the tree became Escher-inspired [carved log tables](https://www.thingelstad.com/2021/06/12/i-love-our.html) for the fire area in 2021, another Rust n’ Grain project. In May 2025 I made the tree the site of [my first location-based POAP](https://www.thingelstad.com/2025/05/25/i-created-my-very-first.html), which visitors could mint by standing beside it.
