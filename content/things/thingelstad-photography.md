By early 2010 Jamie had a portfolio site at photography.thingelstad.com. The [first Internet Archive capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20100224054709/http://photography.thingelstad.com/), from February 2010, sorts his pictures into Featured, Garden, Outdoor and Travel, with type served by Kernest, Garrick van Buren’s web font service.

In July 2012 he [relaunched it](https://www.thingelstad.com/2012/07/01/website-for-my.html) on WordPress with the AutoFocus theme: “I’ve got a new website where I am highlighting my photography, Thingelstad Photography!” He planned to post “an image a week, or more” from his Lightroom archive. The end of the site is not recorded.
