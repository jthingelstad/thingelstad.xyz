By early 2010 I had a portfolio site at photography.thingelstad.com. The [oldest copy in the Internet Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20100224054709/http://photography.thingelstad.com/), from February 2010, sorts my pictures into Featured, Garden, Outdoor and Travel, with type served by Kernest, Garrick van Buren’s web font service.

In July 2012 I [relaunched it](https://www.thingelstad.com/2012/07/01/website-for-my.html) on WordPress with the AutoFocus theme: “I’ve got a new website where I am highlighting my photography, Thingelstad Photography!” The plan was “an image a week, or more” from my Lightroom archive. The site went away quietly at some point. I never wrote about shutting it down.
