By early 2010 Jamie had a portfolio site at photography.thingelstad.com. The [oldest copy in the Internet Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20100224054709/http://photography.thingelstad.com/), from February 2010, sorts his pictures into Featured, Garden, Outdoor and Travel, with type served by Kernest, Garrick van Buren’s web font service.

In July 2012 he [relaunched it](https://www.thingelstad.com/2012/07/01/website-for-my.html) on WordPress with the AutoFocus theme: “I’ve got a new website where I am highlighting my photography, Thingelstad Photography!” The plan was “an image a week, or more” from his Lightroom archive. The site went away quietly at some point. He never wrote about shutting it down, and of the smaller projects of those years he said, [“most of those actually I don’t have a fondness for that much”](https://thingelstad.xyz/interviews/jamie-october-2026/#q26).
