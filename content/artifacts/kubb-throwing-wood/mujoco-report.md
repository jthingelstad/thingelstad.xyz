Generated by `make mjref` (tools/mjref). Ours is core/world on the flat plane with the turf's
dig and bite off ("mech"), the mechanics MuJoCo can model; "game" is the surface as played
(texture, dig, bite; ground seed 1), shown for context. Cells read ours / MuJoCo / game.
D is the case's divergence (0 same, 1 every metric a full scale apart) over the metrics
MuJoCo agrees with itself on across its four solver variants; n/c: none, or a discrete
outcome in doubt, so the case is left out.

## Contact parameters

MuJoCo: elliptic friction cones, impratio 10, Newton solver (200 iterations, tolerance 1e-12),
implicitfast integrator, an explicit contact pair for every pair of geoms. solimp `0.99 0.999 0.001 0.5 2`.
Ground pairs: friction mu of the surface; a baton's pair has condim 6 with torsional
friction roll x batonTurnHold and rolling friction roll (both lengths, as surf.roll is).
Boxes on the ground and every pair of pieces: condim 3, pairMu.
